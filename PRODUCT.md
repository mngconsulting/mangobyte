# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients and collaborators researching MangoByte before reaching out — likely finding the site via a direct link, a search, or word of mouth. They're sizing up whether a two-person software + DevOps studio is senior enough and broad enough (cross-industry experience) to trust with real work. The job to be done: quickly confirm "what do they do, how experienced are they, who are these two people, how do I contact them" — then either bookmark, email, or move on. No account creation, no self-serve funnel; the site's only conversion is a click on an email link.

## Product Purpose

A single-page credibility site for MangoByte (mngconsulting.tech), a two-person, married-couple IT studio in Cluj — one software developer, one DevOps engineer, 7 years combined experience across multiple industries. The site exists to give the company a public presence it currently lacks entirely: it establishes legitimacy, tells the founders' combined story plus individual bios, presents software development and DevOps/infrastructure as one integrated "build it and run it" capability, and makes contact effortless. Success looks like a visitor forming a strong first impression of technical competence within seconds, on any device, and knowing exactly how to get in touch.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, modern, direct. No hype, no filler copy, no generic-consultancy stock-photo feel — the site should read like it was built by people who are actually good at building things, not like a template. Confidence comes from precision (clear claims, real numbers like "7 years combined") rather than adjectives. Voice is plainspoken and a little understated; it lets the founders' expertise and the "build it and run it" framing do the work instead of marketing language.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-consulting-template look: centered hero, gradient-text logo wordmark, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, identical icon+heading+text card grids for services.
- Corporate stock-photo warmth (handshakes, laptops-in-cafes imagery) — this is a two-person studio, not an agency roleplaying as a big firm.
- Anything that reads as "two unrelated freelancers sharing a page" — software dev and DevOps must feel like one integrated offering, and the two founders must read as one studio.
- Cream/sand/beige "warm SaaS" body backgrounds used as a default — if warmth is wanted, it should be a deliberate, committed choice, not the safe default.

## Design Principles

1. **One studio, not two freelancers** — every section (story, bios, services) should reinforce that software + DevOps is a single integrated capability, delivered by two people who work as one unit.
2. **Precision over hype** — let concrete facts (years, industries, what each founder actually does) carry the credibility; avoid vague marketing adjectives.
3. **Branding-agnostic today, swappable tomorrow** — no logo/palette exists yet; the visual system must be built so a real brand palette and logo can drop in later without restructuring markup or components.
4. **Fast and unfussy** — static, no backend, quick to load; the technical craft of the site itself is part of the pitch (a slow or sloppy site undercuts "we build it and run it").
5. **Everyone can read it** — semantic landmarks, real contrast, keyboard and screen-reader access; a credibility site that excludes visitors fails its own purpose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: semantic landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `footer`), body text contrast ≥4.5:1 even against the placeholder palette, fully keyboard-navigable anchor nav, alt text on all images and photo placeholders, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for any motion/animation.
